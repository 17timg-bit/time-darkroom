# 景片来源

## 岬角灯塔

《时间暗房》的原始景片由 Codex 内置 ImageGen 生成。它描绘一处虚构的海岬。应用运行时使用 `assets/cape.js` 中的原始景片和地物遮罩。

### 最终生成提示词

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: base exposure for a fictional interactive photographic instrument, a stationary camera on an isolated North Atlantic headland. Make a widescreen 16:9, full-bleed, extraordinary fine-art large-format landscape photograph. A modest slender off-white lighthouse with a black gallery and glass lantern stands on the pointed end of a dark rugged grassy basalt promontory at approximately 73% across the frame. Its lantern is at 41% down the image. The near headland comes in from the right edge and drops in jagged steep cliffs into a vast still Atlantic ocean which occupies the rest of the lower frame. The horizon is at 49% down the frame and nearly flat, with only tiny distant slatelike islands on the far left. Empty clean ocean foreground, soft white surf threads against the dark rock, no boats. Sky is the upper half, clear open area with very thin diffuse high cloud streaks and a little low sea mist, balanced featureless lighting as if on a cool bright overcast morning. Palette of steel blue-gray, slate, subdued dark olive, silver and pale neutral grays. Absolutely no visible sun, artificial light, stars, or moon, as these will be procedural in the app. Refined archival editorial photography with tactile detail, dramatic sense of space, clear but atmospheric, quietly haunting. Natural photo, no bloom or preexisting film grain. No text, borders, people, logos, or markings.

## 镜湖

原始景片由 Codex 内置 ImageGen 生成，描绘一处虚构的高山湖。应用使用 `assets/lake.js` 中压缩后的原景和地物遮罩。日光、星月、水纹和星月倒影由应用在曝光时绘制。

### 最终生成提示词

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: base exposure of a fictional high alpine glacial lake for a time-slice photographic instrument. Widescreen 16:9 full-bleed fine art large-format landscape, camera almost at the calm waterline. A distant, monumental jagged range of snow-streaked dark stone spires, with its highest steep triangular peak slightly left of center, and its reflected silhouette in the utterly still lake below. Open sky above the mountains and to the right, hazy lower distant peaks at the far right. The mountain foot and far shoreline form a nearly straight horizontal line around 54% down the frame. The water fills the entire bottom 46% from edge to edge. Nothing in the foreground except mirror water, very subtle fine ripples and slightly more blurred reflections near the camera. Very high, fine diffuse cloud wisps in sky. Quiet soft overcast morning, neutral source lighting, alpine steel blue, silver, gray stone, slate, a little snow, subdued turquoise in the water. Beautiful photographic range of values and authentic stone textures, realistic scale and natural cool palette. Camera locked square to horizon, huge sense of space, no wide angle distortion. No sun, moon, stars, night lights, foreground rocks or reeds, wildlife, boats, buildings, roads, text or borders.
